FAQ

Answers, without the noise.

Is this a website app or a real install?

Native apps for Android, Windows, and Mac. Install once — later tap Update in-app.

Who can use TechRen EDU?

Students, teachers, and staff at TechRen education centers with school accounts.

Does it work offline?

You need a connection for sync, leaderboards, and updates. Core practice flows stay fast once loaded.

How do payments reminders work?

From the 1st–10th, unpaid students get polite reminders. They stop after the month is paid.
